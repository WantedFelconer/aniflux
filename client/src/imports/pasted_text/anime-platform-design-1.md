Premium Anime Streaming Platform (Inspired by 9Anime, Netflix, Crunchyroll & Apple)

Objective:
Design a modern, premium, responsive anime streaming platform with an intuitive UX that feels like a combination of Netflix + Crunchyroll + AniList + Apple TV, while maintaining the browsing density of 9Anime.

This should NOT look like a clone of 9Anime. It should feel like the next generation anime streaming website.

The design should be suitable for:

Desktop (1920px)

Tablet (1024px)

Mobile (390px)


Use Auto Layout, Variables, Components, Variants, Design Tokens, and organize everything professionally.

Visual Quality Requirements

Production-ready UI suitable for a commercial streaming platform.

Pixel-perfect alignment using an 8-point grid system.

Consistent spacing, typography, and iconography throughout.

Rich but uncluttered layouts with generous white space where appropriate.

High-quality anime poster placeholders with subtle gradients and shadow depth.

Premium glassmorphism and soft glow effects used sparingly.

Fully componentized with Auto Layout, Variants, Design Tokens, and responsive constraints.

Every screen should feel polished, modern, and cohesive, surpassing the usability and visual quality of 9Anime while preserving its content density and ease of discovery.
